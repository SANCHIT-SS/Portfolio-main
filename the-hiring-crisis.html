The Hiring Crisis: Automated Resume Screening

The Brain

To solve the problem of recruiter "resume fatigue," I engineered a deterministic linear workflow using n8n to orchestrate the backend agent. The core intelligence relies on OpenAI and Anthropic APIs to perform advanced semantic analysis, evaluating candidate context and potential rather than relying on outdated, simple keyword matching. To guarantee stability and prevent the UI from breaking, I utilized prompt engineering to enforce a strict JSON schema output ({"score": number, "summary": string}) from the LLM.

Key Features

  • Automated Semantic Scoring: The agent instantly ingests batch uploads of resumes, compares them against the job description, and assigns a definitive 0-100 match score.
  • Transparent Reasoning Engine: To eliminate decision fatigue, the system is engineered to generate a precise, one-sentence summary explaining exactly why a candidate fits the role.
  • Actionable Leaderboard UI: The Lovable frontend shifts the user experience from "searching" to "validating" by presenting a ranked leaderboard complete with dynamic, one-click action buttons (e.g., "Invite to Interview").

Impact

Drove a 99% time savings by reducing the North Star Metric, "Time-to-Shortlist," from 3 days to under 10 minutes.

Maintained a 90% screening accuracy rate, ensuring high alignment between AI-flagged candidates and human recruiter approval.

Skills & Tools Used

Agentic AI, Lovable, n8n, Python, Prompt Engineering, LLMs.
